Vector Search Algorithms

HNSW (Hierarchical Navigable Small World) is a graph-based algorithm for approximate nearest-neighbor search. It enables fast retrieval of similar vectors.

IVF (Inverted File Index) partitions vectors into clusters. During search, it examines selected clusters to reduce the search space.

Product Quantization (PQ) compresses vectors to reduce memory usage, although compression may reduce retrieval accuracy.

Exact nearest-neighbor search compares a query against every stored vector. It provides exact results but can be expensive for large datasets.

Vector embeddings represent text as numerical vectors. Similarity measures such as cosine similarity help identify related content.
